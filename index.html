WHO WE ARE

Transforming mobility.
Empowering communities.

SPTA’s mission is rooted in transforming the taxi industry into a reliable, efficient, and formalised component of South Africa’s mobility network. By prioritising safety, modernisation, and economic empowerment, SPTA seeks to uplift its members and provide commuters with high-quality transport services. Through strategic partnerships, innovation, and advocacy for fair subsidies, the organisation aims to build a sustainable industry that serves operators, commuters, and the broader economy.

OUR MISSION

Safe, reliable and
innovative mobility.

To drive the transformation of public transport through safe, reliable, and innovative mobility and logistics solutions, while promoting economic empowerment and a diversified, sustainable, subsidized industry that serves all stakeholders.
