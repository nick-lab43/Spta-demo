## Welcome

"Welcome to SPTA, the Seshego Polokwane Taxi Association."

## Organisational introduction

"SPTA’s mission is rooted in transforming the taxi industry into a reliable, efficient, and formalised component of South Africa’s mobility network. By prioritising safety, modernisation, and economic empowerment, SPTA seeks to uplift its members and provide commuters with high-quality transport services. Through strategic partnerships, innovation, and advocacy for fair subsidies, the organisation aims to build a sustainable industry that serves operators, commuters, and the broader economy."

## Mission

"To drive the transformation of public transport through safe, reliable, and innovative mobility and logistics solutions, while promoting economic empowerment and a diversified, sustainable, subsidized industry that serves all stakeholders."

- Driving Transformation

## Values

The client describes SPTA's values as the foundation of its identity, guiding actions, decisions and relationships across the public transport sector. They reflect a commitment to professionalism, safety, empowerment, inclusive representation and broader economic participation.

### Empowerment

"We are committed to enhancing economic opportunities for our members and employees across the transport value chain."

Practical commitments:
- Facilitate access to finance, training, and business development support.
- Foster a culture of self-reliance and growth through opportunity.
- Support inclusive participation in enterprise development and industry transformation.

### Professionalism

"We uphold the highest standards of organisation, accountability, and professional conduct within the taxi industry."

Practical commitments:
- Enforce codes of conduct and continuous skills development.
- Promote discipline, respect, and commitment among all stakeholders.
- Maintain robust governance structures and performance accountability mechanisms.

### Safety

"We are dedicated to ensuring safe, reliable, and secure transport for all commuters and operators."

Practical commitments:
- Run driver training programs, vehicle inspections, and safety awareness campaigns.
- Enforce compliance with national safety standards and promote best practices.
- Cultivate a safety-first mindset throughout the industry.

### Involvement

"We are committed to expanding the taxi industry’s role and presence in the broader economy through active participation."

Practical commitments:
- Promote innovation and diversification beyond traditional operations.
- Encourage participation in logistics, infrastructure, and technology sectors.
- Drive member-owned ventures and partnerships.

### Representation & Commitment

"We advocate for our members’ interests with integrity, ensuring every voice is heard and our mission upheld."

Practical commitments:
- Engage stakeholders across all levels and platforms.
- Build transparent, democratic structures that support collective decision-making.
- Lead with unity, purpose, and accountability.
