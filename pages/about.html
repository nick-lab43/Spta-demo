OUR ROLE

Part of the local
transport network.

The Seshego Polokwane Taxi Association is identified in Polokwane public transport planning as one of the local taxi associations involved in the area's public transport environment.

Municipal documentation describes the association's involvement in planning and operating arrangements connected to the Leeto La Polokwane Integrated Public Transport System.

This is a researched introductory summary, not an official statement of SPTA's mission or constitution. The association should approve its official history, leadership, mission and vision before publication.

OUR IDENTITY

Together as One.

The phrase appears in the association's supplied logo and is retained here as its existing slogan.
