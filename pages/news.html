18 MAY 2026 THIRD-PARTY REPORT

Reported fare adjustment on the Seshego–Polokwane journey

SABC News reported that SPTA announced an adjustment from R15 to R20, effective 18 May 2026. This is a historical report, not confirmation of the fare currently charged.
